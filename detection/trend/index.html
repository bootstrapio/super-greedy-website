What Is Trend Detection?

Trend Detection finds high-probability directional trades. When a clear trend and valid setup appears, Super Greedy detects the setup, places the order, and manages the trade from entry to exit.

  1. Trend

    Detects overall market direction to identify long or short trade opportunities.

  2. EMA

    Adds trend confirmation using moving averages before a setup becomes valid.

FAQ

Trend Detection Questions

Learn how Super Greedy trend detection works, which futures markets it supports, and how automated trading and risk management work.

How does Super Greedy detect trend setups?

⌄

Super Greedy automatically detects trend trading setups using trend direction, EMA confirmation, and configurable market conditions. When a valid setup appears, the bot can detect the trade, prepare entry and risk levels, and manage the trade lifecycle automatically.

Can I customize trend detection settings?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy lets you customize trend detection settings, including EMA filters, trading sessions, directional bias, entry behavior, stop loss, take profit, and risk management settings directly from the dashboard.

Which futures markets does trend detection support?

⌄

Trend Detection supports ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ futures contracts. Super Greedy can monitor setups, place trades, and manage positions automatically across supported futures markets.

Does Super Greedy work with TopstepX accounts?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy integrates with TopstepX for automated futures trading on practice, combine, and funded accounts. Once connected, the bot can detect setups, place orders, and manage trades automatically based on your configured strategy.

Does Super Greedy manage risk automatically?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy automatically manages risk using bracket orders with stop loss and take profit levels. The bot can also manage open trades based on your configured rules, helping automate trade management from entry to exit.
