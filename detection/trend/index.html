What Is Trend Detection?

Trend Detection is built for traders who want to follow the market's direction. Super Greedy looks for clean momentum setups, prepares entry and risk levels, and runs the full trade for you — from first signal through exit.

  1. Trend

    The default mode. Super Greedy reads market direction and looks for high-probability entries when momentum is clear.

  2. EMA

    Optional confirmation with moving averages. Turn it on when you want stricter trend-following filters before a setup qualifies.

FAQ

Trend Detection Questions

Quick answers about automated trend trading with Super Greedy.

What is Super Greedy Trend Detection?

⌄

Super Greedy Trend Detection is automated futures trading software that finds directional trade setups when the market is trending. It detects the opportunity, places the order, sets stop loss and take profit levels, and manages the trade from entry through exit.

How does Super Greedy detect trend setups?

⌄

Super Greedy continuously scans for clear upward or downward movement and qualifies setups based on your detection settings. You can run the default trend mode or turn on EMA confirmation for stricter filtering. When a setup passes, the bot prepares the trade and takes it from there.

Can I customize trend detection settings?

⌄

Yes. You can toggle EMA confirmation, choose trading sessions, set directional bias, and adjust stop loss, take profit, and risk rules directly from the Super Greedy dashboard.

Which futures markets does trend detection support?

⌄

Trend Detection works on major US futures markets Super Greedy supports, including ES, NQ, MES, MNQ, and other index, metals, and energy contracts. It runs on single-chart, multi-timeframe, and multi-contract layouts.

Does Super Greedy work with TopstepX accounts?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy connects to TopstepX for automated futures trading on practice, combine, and funded accounts. Tradovate is supported too. Once connected, the bot can detect setups, place orders, and manage trades based on your strategy.

Does Super Greedy manage risk automatically?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy places bracket orders with stop loss and take profit levels, manages open positions automatically, and can cancel or exit trades when market conditions no longer support the setup.
