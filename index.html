Frequently Asked Questions

What you should know before you start.

Straight answers about how Super Greedy works, what it costs, and what to expect.

What is Super Greedy?

⌄

Super Greedy is automated futures trading software for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ. It monitors live market conditions, follows your configured strategy settings, and executes trades automatically — entry, stop, and targets — so you don't have to watch the chart.

Does Super Greedy trade automatically without manual intervention?

⌄

Yes. Once configured, Super Greedy detects setups, validates entries against your rules, and places the full trade — entry, stop, and take-profit targets — with no manual action required.

How much does Super Greedy cost?

⌄

Super Greedy is $75 per month. That covers automated trading across practice, combine, and funded accounts, plus the TopstepX API integration at no extra charge.

Do I need a separate TopstepX API subscription?

⌄

No. The TopstepX API (normally $30/mo) is included in the Super Greedy subscription. You only need an active TopstepX account to connect.

How does Super Greedy detect setups?

⌄

Super Greedy evaluates live price action bar-by-bar using your enabled detection modes — Trend, ORB, or Bullets — and only acts when conditions match your configured rules.

What futures markets does Super Greedy support?

⌄

Super Greedy is built for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ futures. Additional instruments are configurable depending on your setup and broker availability.

Can I customize the risk and execution parameters?

⌄

Yes. Risk per trade, position size, daily loss limits, drawdown thresholds, and take-profit targets are all configurable so every execution fits your account rules and trading plan.

Does Super Greedy work with prop firm evaluation rules?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy includes configurable daily loss limits, max drawdown thresholds, and position sizing controls built to align with TopstepX and common prop firm evaluation requirements.

One Last Thing

Every setup you miss is an edge you left behind.

Your strategy works when it's applied consistently. Super Greedy keeps it running — every session, every setup, every bar — so consistency isn't something you have to maintain manually.
