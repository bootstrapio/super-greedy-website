Frequently Asked Questions

What you should know before you start.

Straight answers about how Super Greedy works, what it costs, and what to expect.

What is Super Greedy?

⌄

Super Greedy is automated futures trading software for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ. It monitors live market conditions with multi-pane charts and your configured strategy settings so you can trade with consistent context.

Does Super Greedy place trades automatically?

⌄

No. Super Greedy is a signal-only chart dashboard. It streams live market data and keeps your strategy settings visible, but you control every entry and exit.

How much does Super Greedy cost?

⌄

Super Greedy is $75 per month. That covers the dashboard across practice, combine, and funded accounts, plus the TopstepX API integration at no extra charge.

Do I need a separate TopstepX API subscription?

⌄

No. The TopstepX API (normally $30/mo) is included in the Super Greedy subscription. You only need an active TopstepX account to connect.

What chart layouts does Super Greedy support?

⌄

Super Greedy supports multi-timeframe, multi-contract, and stocks layouts. Each pane can use the intervals and symbols you configure in the Strategy card.

What futures markets does Super Greedy support?

⌄

Super Greedy is built for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ futures. Additional instruments are configurable depending on your setup and broker availability.

Can I customize the risk and execution parameters?

⌄

Yes. Position size is configurable so your dashboard reflects your trading plan.

Does Super Greedy work with prop firm evaluation rules?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy works with TopstepX practice, evaluation, and funded accounts, and supports Tradovate as well. Position sizing and risk settings help you stay consistent with prop firm evaluation requirements.

One Last Thing

Every setup you miss is an edge you left behind.

Your strategy works when it's applied consistently. Super Greedy keeps your charts and settings running — every session, every pane, every bar — so context isn't something you have to rebuild manually.
