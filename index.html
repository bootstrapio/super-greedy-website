Frequently Asked Questions

What you should know before you start.

Straight answers about how Super Greedy works, what it costs, and what to expect.

What is Super Greedy?

⌄

Super Greedy is automated futures trading software for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ. It runs one Trend detection strategy across your live charts and executes it for you, inside the sessions, bias, and risk limits you configure.

Does Super Greedy place trades automatically?

⌄

Yes. When the Trend setup appears inside your configured session and bias, Super Greedy submits the entry with its stop loss and take profit attached, and manages the position through to the exit. You set the limits; it does the execution.

Can I choose a different strategy?

⌄

No. Super Greedy runs a single Trend detection strategy. Instead of a menu of strategies you'd have to evaluate yourself, you get one that's tuned and executed consistently.

How much does Super Greedy cost?

⌄

Super Greedy is $75 per month. That covers automated trading across practice, combine, and funded accounts, plus the TopstepX API integration at no extra charge.

Do I need a separate TopstepX API subscription?

⌄

No. The TopstepX API (normally $30/mo) is included in the Super Greedy subscription. You only need an active TopstepX account to connect.

What chart layouts does Super Greedy support?

⌄

Super Greedy supports multi-timeframe, multi-contract, and stocks layouts. Each pane can use the intervals and symbols you configure in the Strategy card, and each pane can trade.

What futures markets does Super Greedy support?

⌄

Super Greedy is built for ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ futures. Additional instruments are configurable depending on your setup and broker availability.

Can I customize the risk and execution parameters?

⌄

Yes — contracts, timeframe, exit type, and max stop loss. What you can't do is change the strategy itself, and that's deliberate: one strategy, traded the same way every time, is what makes results comparable.

Does Super Greedy work with prop firm evaluation rules?

⌄

Yes. Super Greedy works with TopstepX practice, evaluation, and funded accounts, and supports Tradovate as well. Position sizing and max stop loss help you stay inside prop firm evaluation requirements.

One Last Thing

The setups you miss are the ones you were away for.

Super Greedy doesn't get tired, distracted, or talked out of a trade. It watches every pane, every bar, and takes the setup the moment it's there.
