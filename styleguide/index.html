Color tokens

Six marketing colors, referenced everywhere.

Non-chart marketing UI resolves to the six RGB palette tokens below. Chart illustration colors are separate and mirror the app chart palette. The embedded Platform preview scopes application dark tokens under a nested .dark surface.

--marketing-color-white Page background and text on accent surfaces.
--marketing-color-gray-50 Carousel cards, chips, and control surfaces.
--marketing-color-gray-200 Borders, dividers, and muted text.
--marketing-color-ink Primary text and ink surfaces.
--marketing-color-accent Links, CTAs, and emphasis.
--marketing-color-black Shadows (via color-mix).
--chart-bar-neutral Neutral chart bar; mirrored from the app chart palette.
--chart-bar-down Down chart bar; mirrored from the app chart palette.
--chart-bar-long Long chart bar; mirrored from the app chart palette.
--chart-bar-open Open chart bar; mirrored from the app chart palette.
--chart-illustration-background Chart illustration background.
--chart-illustration-take-profit Take-profit line and label color.
--chart-illustration-stop-loss Stop-loss line and label color.
--chart-illustration-take-profit-plate Take-profit label plate.
--chart-illustration-entry-plate Entry label plate.
--chart-illustration-stop-loss-plate Stop-loss label plate.

Embedded app palette

The Platform card deliberately scopes the shared app dark tokens under its own .dark container so its preview remains faithful without changing the marketing page palette.

app --background Scoped app dark surface.
app --foreground Scoped app text.
app --primary Scoped app primary action.
app --card Scoped app card surface.

Success, warning, error, and info colors remain app-owned state semantics; marketing surfaces do not invent a second state palette.

Motion

Motion has a shared owner and a reduced-motion rule.

CTA icons, the pricing arrow, chart illustration, and embedded account values use named stylesheet animations. Reduced-motion preferences remove continuous movement where the component owns it.

  • cta-icon-spincta-icon-spinPrimary CTA icon rotation.
  • section-link-caret-driftsection-link-caret-driftLive pricing CTA arrow drift.
  • chartBarInchartBarInChart bar reveal.
  • chartDotInchartDotInChart signal reveal.
  • chartDotPulsechartDotPulseChart signal pulse.
  • chartLevelInchartLevelInChart level reveal.
  • gbMktValueTickgbMktValueTickEmbedded Platform value ticker.

The reduced-motion media rule disables the embedded value ticker; the chart and CTA motion remains declarative and finite or can be removed by the browser preference.
